Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This site does not use cookies

As of this build, Solus does not set any cookies. Not for analytics, not for advertising, not for remembering preferences, nothing. That's also why there's no cookie-consent banner on this site: under most privacy regulations (including India's DPDP Act and the EU's ePrivacy rules), a consent banner is required when a site sets non-essential cookies. Since none are set here, there's nothing to ask permission for, and adding a banner anyway would just be noise for no reason.

What about Google Fonts?

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Google's font-delivery infrastructure is specifically built not to use cookies or log requests in a way that's tied to a Google account. So this doesn't introduce cookies either.

What would change this

If Solus ever adds something that genuinely needs cookies (visitor analytics to understand traffic, for instance, or a login system for members), this page will be updated first, and a proper consent banner will go up alongside that change, before it ships. We're not going to quietly start tracking people and leave this page saying "no cookies."

Browser-level control

Even though this site doesn't use cookies, most browsers let you block or clear cookies site-by-site if you want extra peace of mind in general web browsing, usually under Settings → Privacy in whichever browser you're using.
