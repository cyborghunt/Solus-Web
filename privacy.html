1. The short version

This site does not run analytics, does not use advertising or tracking pixels, does not have user accounts or logins, and does not sell, share, or store personal data on a server anywhere. There's genuinely very little to this policy because there's very little data collection happening. See the Cookie Policy for the cookie-specific breakdown.

2. The introduction-request form

The form on the Access page asks for a name, the member who knows you, your machine, and a short note. As this site currently stands, submitting that form does not transmit anything to a server. It displays a simulated confirmation entirely inside your own browser, and nothing you type is saved, sent, or stored anywhere. If this ever changes (for example, if the form starts emailing submissions to the club), this page will be updated first to say exactly what's collected and where it goes.

3. Fonts and external resources

This site loads its typefaces (Anton, Inter, and IBM Plex Mono) from Google Fonts. Google's font-serving infrastructure is built not to use cookies or require a Google account to deliver font files. No other third-party scripts, trackers, or embeds are loaded by this site.

4. Photos of people

Photos across this site, on the homepage, the drive archive, and the gallery, are taken at Solus drives and meetups, generally of club members and their machines. If you appear in a photo here and would rather it weren't used, reach out through Instagram and it will be taken down.

5. Your rights

Because this site doesn't currently collect or store personal data, there isn't a database entry to request access to or delete. If that changes in the future, this section will be updated to explain how to exercise those rights (access, correction, deletion) over whatever is actually being stored by then.

6. Children's privacy

This site isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

7. Changes to this policy

If what this site collects ever changes (adding analytics, wiring the access form to a real inbox, anything like that), this page will be updated to reflect it before that change goes live, not after.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through Instagram for now.
