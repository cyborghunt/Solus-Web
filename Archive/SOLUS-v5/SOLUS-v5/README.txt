SOLUS V4 — WEBSITE CONCEPT

1. Open index.html in Chrome/Edge.
2. Use MENU + for navigation.
3. In THE GALLERY, click LOAD YOUR GALLERY + and select an entire folder of images.
   You can also drag/drop image files anywhere on the page.
4. Click any gallery image to open it full-screen.
5. ACCESS on the homepage opens access.html, where KNOW A MEMBER opens the working demo introduction form.

For a production site, replace the concept images in assets/gallery/ with the real Solus photography/video and connect the introduction form to a real backend/email service.
